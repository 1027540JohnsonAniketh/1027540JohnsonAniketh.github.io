What this income buys in Lubbock

Lubbock is a low-cost city: a C2ER-based index puts it about 10% below the US average overall and 16% below on housing. On $86,736 a year, rent is unlikely to be the constraint.

Apartments

Thirteen communities with current advertised rents, sorted by straight-line distance from UMC. Prices are listing snapshots from 13–14 Oct 2026, so treat them as advertised ranges to confirm with the property; fees, specials and income screens (usually 3× rent) apply.

Groceries

Costco, Sam’s Club, the six Lubbock Walmarts found on Walmart’s store pages (five Supercenters and a Neighborhood Market), H-E-B, United/Market Street, Sprouts and Natural Grocers. Lubbock had no Aldi as of the latest report found (2022), and this research found no Trader Joe’s or Whole Foods either; confirm before counting on them.

Indian groceries

Lubbock has one dedicated Indian grocery, Mirch Masala on 34th Street, plus an international market on 50th; H-E-B and the Walmart Supercenters carry basic South Asian staples, and online grocers such as Swagat Grocery advertise delivery to Lubbock.

Restaurants & coffee

A representative mix: three Indian restaurants, Texas barbecue, Tex-Mex, a steakhouse, Thai, a date-night kitchen, a breakfast institution and two coffee shops near campus. Menu prices are snapshots and change.

Recreation & culture

Museums, music, parks, a lake and wineries, all inside the 50-mile circle. Palo Duro Canyon and Caprock Canyons are popular day trips but lie beyond 50 miles, so they are not mapped.

Healthcare

UMC is Lubbock’s 500-bed Level I trauma center and the primary teaching hospital for Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center; Covenant, Grace and Lubbock Heart & Surgical round out the hospital system, with urgent care and a VA clinic on 4th Street.

Shopping

South Plains Mall anchors retail at Slide Road and Loop 289; the Canyon West cluster on Marsha Sharp Freeway holds Costco, Sam’s Club, Target, Academy and Best Buy within a mile of each other.

Method, accuracy and limits

Pins and distances

Every address was geocoded with the US Census Bureau Geocoder (TIGER address ranges) and cross-checked against a second TIGER benchmark or an independent source; most pairs agree within 100 m. Where the geocoder had no range for an address, the pin comes from the business’s own map link, a directory’s map coordinates or Wikipedia, and the popup says so. One pin (Evie Mae’s, Wolfforth) is approximate to about half a mile and is drawn dashed.

“Straight-line” is the great-circle distance from UMC. “Drive” is an OpenStreetMap-based OSRM estimate for the fastest car route; real trips vary with traffic and the route you pick.

Basemap

Drawn from open data so the geometry is real, not sketched: Natural Earth 10m highways, county lines, rivers and built-up areas; Census TIGERweb for Lubbock’s mile-grid arterials; GeoNames for towns; the 50-mile ring is geodesic. The interactive map is vector-only (no third-party tiles), so it also works offline once loaded. The PNGs use an azimuthal equidistant projection centred on UMC, so distances from the centre are true to scale.

What this is not

A researched selection, not a directory: Lubbock has more apartments, restaurants and clinics than appear here. Prices and hours were retrieved on 14 Oct 2026 from the sources listed and will drift. Confirm rents, availability and fees with each property before deciding.
